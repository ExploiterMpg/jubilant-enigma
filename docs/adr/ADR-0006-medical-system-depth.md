# ADR-0006 — Medical System Depth

Status: Accepted, incomplete

## Context

Current medical decisions:

- Body-part health exists.
- Bleeding exists.
- Medkits have durability.
- Medkits heal over time.
- Blacked limbs require a Surgical Kit.
- Surgical Kit takes 10 seconds and restores a blacked limb to 1 HP.
- Head/thorax blacking remains lethal.

## Incomplete details

The following are not fully designed:

- Pain effects.
- Movement penalties for blacked legs.
- Aim/recoil penalties for blacked arms.
- Heavy vs light bleeding.
- Fractures.
- Different medkit tiers.
- Whether surgery reduces max HP until extraction.

## Recommendation

Keep the current system simple until combat feels good.

Next useful additions:

- Blacked leg slows movement.
- Blacked arm reduces accuracy/fire rate.
- Painkillers temporarily ignore movement penalties.
- Better medkits heal faster or have more durability.
